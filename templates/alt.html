Quick guide to good alt text
  • Say what the image tells a student, not everything in it. One or two sentences, ideally under 250 characters.
  • Don't start with "Image of". Screen readers already announce it as an image.
  • Charts: the type and the main point ("Bar chart: enrollment rose each year from 2022 to 2025").
  • Logos: the organization's name plus "logo". Photos of people: who they are, if that matters.
  • Decorative means it adds nothing (borders, background art). Screen readers will skip it.
  • Complex charts, diagrams or equations need a longer description in the document too, such as a paragraph or data table right after the image.
